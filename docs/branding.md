# Telomy logo

The source icon is stored at `assets/branding/telomy-icon.png` and resized for Android launcher densities and every iOS AppIcon slot. The same asset appears in onboarding, splash, profile, and the dashboard.

Prompt: “Use case: logo-brand. Create a final 1024x1024 square mobile launcher icon for TELOMY, a premium personal longevity and fitness tracking app. No lettering. A distinctive simple ivory continuous-loop symbol combining a soft infinity loop with an abstract L, representing a longitudinal health journey and a connected smart ring. Centered bold geometric mark, legible at 32px, occupy central 60% of canvas. Solid deep forest teal background #123F3A filling every pixel to edges, small mint accent integrated into the loop. Flat vector-like crisp shapes, exquisite balanced spacing, no mockup, no border, no shadows, no photographic textures. Opaque square image, no rounded outer corners; operating systems apply their own mask.”
